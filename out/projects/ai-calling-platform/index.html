Problem

Manual outbound calling is time-consuming and inefficient. Sales teams struggle to qualify leads at scale, resulting in wasted time on unqualified prospects.

Solution

Built an automated outbound calling platform using Vapi voice agents powered by LLMs for lead qualification. Integrated with HubSpot CRM for lead management and Twilio for voice communication, enabling automated, personalized outreach at scale.
